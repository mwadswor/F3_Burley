New to F3

Who are we?

F3 is a network of guys who run free, outdoor, peer-led workouts for men across the nation. Our region operates in Burley and the surrounding area — out on the fields and trails before the sun's up.

The mission of F3 is to plant, serve, and grow small workout groups for men for the invigoration of male community leadership.

What Makes It F3

The Five Core Principles

A workout is an F3 workout only if it abides by these principles.

01

Free of charge.

No membership fee, no dues, nothing. You earn your free lifetime membership by showing up. For an extra discount, use code ‘FNG’ (Friendly New Guy) at your first workout for an additional 10% off the initial $0 registration fee!

02

Open to all men.

Seriously, it isn't a competition. All you need is the desire and willingness to challenge yourself.

03

Held outdoors.

Workouts don't require any special equipment, and we don't shy away from inclement weather.

04

Peer-led.

There's no meathead with a clipboard. The men who attend step up to lead, and the leader works out alongside you.

05

Circle of Trust.

Every workout ends with the CoT, where men can share their struggles and encourage one another.
